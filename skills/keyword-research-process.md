# Keyword Research Process - Bright Forge SEO

## Phase 1: Discovery & Brainstorming

### Understand the Business
- [ ] Client's products/services
- [ ] Target audience demographics
- [ ] Geographic markets (AU/UK/etc)
- [ ] Unique selling propositions
- [ ] Business goals and KPIs
- [ ] Current rankings (if any)

### Seed Keywords
- [ ] Primary services/products
- [ ] Industry terms
- [ ] Brand terms
- [ ] Competitor brand terms
- [ ] Problem/solution keywords
- [ ] Question-based keywords

### Initial Keyword Lists
- [ ] Main topics (5-10 topics)
- [ ] 10-20 seed keywords per topic
- [ ] Client-provided keywords
- [ ] Common industry terminology

## Phase 2: Keyword Expansion

### Tools to Use
- Google Keyword Planner
- Ahrefs Keywords Explorer
- SEMrush Keyword Magic Tool
- Google Search Console (existing data)
- Answer The Public
- Google autocomplete
- Related searches

### Expansion Methods

**1. Seed Keyword Expansion:**
- Enter each seed keyword into tools
- Export all related keywords
- Minimum 100 keywords per topic

**2. Competitor Analysis:**
- Identify top 5 competitors
- Extract their ranking keywords
- Find gaps where they rank but client doesn't
- Identify opportunities

**3. Question Keywords:**
- "How to" queries
- "What is" queries
- "Why" queries
- "Best" queries
- Problem-solution queries

**4. Long-Tail Variations:**
- Location modifiers (if local)
- Product/service modifiers
- Intent modifiers (buy, hire, cheap, best)
- Year/season modifiers

## Phase 3: Keyword Metrics Analysis

### Key Metrics to Track
- **Search Volume:** Monthly searches
- **Keyword Difficulty:** Competition level
- **CPC:** Cost per click (indicator of commercial value)
- **Search Intent:** Informational/Navigational/Commercial/Transactional
- **SERP Features:** Featured snippets, PAA, local pack, etc.
- **Current Rankings:** If client already ranks

### Data Collection
```
Keyword | Volume | Difficulty | CPC | Intent | SERP Features | Current Rank
--------|--------|------------|-----|--------|---------------|-------------
[keyword] | [vol] | [KD] | [cpc] | [intent] | [features] | [rank]
```

## Phase 4: Keyword Categorization

### By Search Intent
**Informational:**
- Blog content targets
- Guide/resource targets
- Low immediate conversion
- Top of funnel

**Commercial Investigation:**
- Comparison pages
- "Best" lists
- Reviews
- Middle of funnel

**Transactional:**
- Service pages
- Product pages
- "Hire" / "Buy" keywords
- Bottom of funnel

**Navigational:**
- Brand keywords
- Specific page searches

### By Priority
**High Priority:**
- High volume + low/medium difficulty
- High commercial intent
- Currently ranking 11-30
- Quick win potential

**Medium Priority:**
- Medium volume + medium difficulty
- Mixed intent
- Strategic importance
- Medium-term targets

**Low Priority:**
- Low volume OR high difficulty
- Nice to have
- Long-term targets
- Very specific queries

## Phase 5: Keyword Grouping & Mapping

### Topic Clusters
Group keywords into clusters:

**Cluster Example: "Scaffolding Hire"**
- Main keyword: scaffolding hire [1000/mo, KD 45]
- Supporting keywords:
  - scaffolding rental [500/mo, KD 40]
  - hire scaffolding near me [300/mo, KD 35]
  - temporary scaffolding hire [200/mo, KD 30]
  - scaffold tower hire [150/mo, KD 35]

**Pillar Page:** Main service page
**Cluster Content:** 4-6 supporting articles

### Content Mapping
| URL/Page Type | Primary Keyword | Secondary Keywords | Search Volume | Priority |
|---------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| /services/scaffolding-hire | scaffolding hire | scaffold rental, temporary scaffolding | 1000 | High |
| /blog/scaffolding-safety | scaffolding safety tips | safe scaffold use, scaffold regulations | 500 | Medium |

## Phase 6: Competitor Keyword Gap Analysis

### Process
1. Identify top 3-5 competitors
2. Extract their ranking keywords
3. Filter for keywords where they rank top 10
4. Identify keywords client doesn't rank for
5. Prioritize based on:
   - Search volume
   - Relevance
   - Difficulty
   - Commercial value

### Gap Opportunities
**High-Value Gaps:**
- Competitors rank top 3
- Client doesn't rank
- Medium-high volume
- Achievable difficulty

**Quick Win Gaps:**
- Competitors rank 4-10
- Client doesn't rank
- Low-medium difficulty
- Can outrank with better content

## Phase 7: SERP Analysis

### For Each Target Keyword

**Analyze Top 10 Results:**
- [ ] Page types ranking (blog/service/product)
- [ ] Content length average
- [ ] Content quality assessment
- [ ] Domain authority of competitors
- [ ] SERP features present
- [ ] User intent match
- [ ] Content gaps/weaknesses

**SERP Features:**
- Featured snippet opportunity?
- People Also Ask boxes
- Image pack
- Video results
- Local pack
- Shopping results

**Competitive Assessment:**
- Can we create better content?
- Do we have domain authority?
- What's missing in current results?
- What angle can we take?

## Phase 8: Final Keyword Selection

### Selection Criteria
**Include if:**
- Relevant to business
- Achievable difficulty for domain
- Sufficient search volume
- Clear content opportunity
- Matches business goals

**Exclude if:**
- Irrelevant to business
- Too difficult for current domain
- Very low/no search volume
- Poor ROI potential
- Negative/irrelevant intent

### Final Deliverables

**Primary Target Keywords (10-20):**
- Highest priority
- Service/product pages
- 6-12 month targets

**Secondary Target Keywords (50-100):**
- Blog content topics
- Supporting pages
- 12-24 month targets

**Long-Tail Keywords (200+):**
- Article variations
- FAQ content
- Ongoing content bank
