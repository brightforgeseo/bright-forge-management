# Content Creation Standards - Bright Forge SEO

## Article Quality Requirements

### Writing Standards
- Professional expertise tone
- NO AI-generated language patterns
- Avoid phrases like:
  - "In today's digital landscape"
  - "It's important to note that"
  - "In conclusion"
  - "Delve into"
  - "Leverage"
  - Generic AI-sounding transitions
- Data-driven statements with specifics
- Actionable insights, not generic advice

### Structure Requirements
- Clear H2 and H3 hierarchy
- Scannable paragraphs (3-4 sentences max)
- Bullet points for lists where appropriate
- Natural flow between sections
- Strong intro hook
- Practical conclusion with takeaways

### Length Guidelines
- Standard articles: 1,500-2,500 words
- Pillar content: 3,000-5,000 words
- Service pages: 800-1,200 words
- Product descriptions: 300-500 words

## Quality Assurance Checklist
- [ ] Keyword integration verified (see keyword-integration.md)
- [ ] Internal links added per strategy (see linking-strategy.md)
- [ ] No repeated patterns from previous articles
- [ ] Statistics and data included where relevant
- [ ] Original sources cited
- [ ] Brand voice maintained
- [ ] Meta title and description optimized
- [ ] Images/media specified if needed
