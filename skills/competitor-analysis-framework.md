# Competitor Analysis Framework - Bright Forge SEO

## Identifying Competitors

### Types of Competitors

**1. Direct Competitors**
- Same products/services
- Same target audience
- Same geographic area
- Direct business competition

**2. SEO Competitors**
- Rank for same keywords
- May not be direct business competitors
- Compete for same SERP space
- Similar content topics

**3. Aspirational Competitors**
- Industry leaders
- Where client wants to be
- Benchmark for success

### How to Identify SEO Competitors

**Method 1: Keyword-Based**
1. Enter your target keywords in Google
2. Note who ranks in top 10
3. Identify patterns (same sites ranking for multiple keywords)
4. List top 3-5 consistent competitors

**Method 2: Tool-Based**
1. Use Ahrefs/SEMrush "Competitors" feature
2. Enter client's domain
3. Review suggested competitors
4. Filter by relevance and overlap

**Method 3: Client Input**
- Ask client who their main competitors are
- Who do they lose business to?
- Who do customers compare them to?
