# Linking Strategy & Variation Framework - Bright Forge SEO

## CRITICAL LINKING RULES

### Universal Rules
1. **No service URL appears 2x in same article**
2. **No repeated anchor text across articles**
3. **No repeated link positions across articles**
4. **Vary strategy batch to batch**

### Link Distribution Per Article

**Minimum Requirements:**
- Homepage: 1 link
- Primary service page: 1-2 links
- Related service pages: 2-3 links
- Blog/resource links: 1-2 links
- Total: 5-8 internal links per article

**Maximum Limits:**
- Never more than 1 link to same URL per article
- Never more than 2 links in same paragraph
- Avoid linking in first 100 words (rare exceptions only)

## Variation Strategies

### Batch 1 Strategy: Early CTA Focus
- Homepage link in intro (paragraph 2-3)
- Service links distributed in H3 sections
- Related blog links near conclusion
- Strong CTA toward end

### Batch 2 Strategy: Mid-Content Service Emphasis
- No homepage link until mid-article
- Service links in different H3 positions than Batch 1
- Blog links in body, not conclusion
- CTA placement varied

### Batch 3 Strategy: Natural Distribution
- Homepage in middle sections
- Service links in intro and conclusion
- Blog links throughout body
- Multiple smaller CTAs

### Batch 4 Strategy: Conclusion-Heavy
- Minimal intro links
- Service concentration in final third
- Homepage near end
- Summary-style linking

## Anchor Text Variation

### Primary Service Anchors (rotate these):
- "scaffolding services"
- "professional scaffolding solutions"
- "expert scaffolding hire"
- "reliable scaffolding providers"
- "scaffolding experts"

### Homepage Anchors (rotate these):
- Company name
- "our team"
- "learn more"
- "discover more"
- Brief branded phrase

### Context-Specific Anchors:
- Use surrounding context to inform anchor
- Action-oriented when appropriate
- Natural reading flow
- Never generic "click here"

## Link Density Guidelines
- 1-2 links per 200 words (rough guide)
- Higher density acceptable in resource-heavy sections
- Lower density in storytelling sections
- Never cluster 3+ links together

## Verification Before Publishing
- [ ] No duplicate URLs in article
- [ ] Anchor text different from last 5 articles
- [ ] Link positions vary from recent articles
- [ ] Homepage link strategy differs from previous batch
- [ ] Service page distribution is unique
- [ ] Total link count within 5-8 range
