# Link Building Outreach Templates - Bright Forge SEO

## General Outreach Best Practices

### Before Sending
- [ ] Verify email address is correct
- [ ] Personalize the email (name, website, recent content)
- [ ] Check their site for link placement opportunities
- [ ] Ensure your content is genuinely valuable
- [ ] Have a clear value proposition
- [ ] Keep email concise (under 150 words)
- [ ] One clear call-to-action

### Email Structure
1. Personalized greeting
2. Brief compliment/connection (1-2 sentences)
3. Value proposition (what's in it for them)
4. Specific ask
5. Make it easy (provide link, anchor text)
6. Professional sign-off

## Best Practices & Tips

### Subject Line Tips
- Keep under 50 characters
- Personalize when possible
- Be specific (mention their site/content)
- Avoid spammy words (free, guarantee, act now)
- Make it relevant to them, not you

### Personalization Checklist
- [ ] Use their actual name (not "Hi there")
- [ ] Mention specific content from their site
- [ ] Show you actually visited and read
- [ ] Reference something recent they published
- [ ] Tailor pitch to their audience

### Red Flags to Avoid
- ❌ Generic "Dear Webmaster"
- ❌ Mass-email feel
- ❌ No value for them
- ❌ Aggressive or pushy tone
- ❌ Too long (over 200 words)
- ❌ Multiple asks in one email
- ❌ Spelling their name wrong
- ❌ Spelling errors in general

### Response Rate Optimization
- Send during business hours (their timezone)
- Tuesday-Thursday typically best
- Follow up once after 4-7 days
- A/B test subject lines
- Track open and response rates
- Refine based on what works

### When to Stop Outreaching
- After 2 follow-ups with no response
- If they explicitly say no
- If they mark as spam
- If their site quality is poor
- If they have no-follow links policy

## Legal & Ethics

### Always:
- ✅ Be honest about your intentions
- ✅ Respect their time
- ✅ Accept "no" gracefully
- ✅ Follow anti-spam laws (CAN-SPAM, GDPR)
- ✅ Provide unsubscribe option for newsletters
- ✅ Only contact business emails

### Never:
- ❌ Buy email lists
- ❌ Use deceptive subject lines
- ❌ Hide your intentions
- ❌ Harass after "no"
- ❌ Offer to pay for links (violates Google guidelines)
- ❌ Use automated scraping without permission
