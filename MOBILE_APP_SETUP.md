# Get the Portal on Your Phone

No app store needed. Pick your phone type below.

## Android

1. On the phone, open this link in Chrome:
   **https://github.com/brightforgeseo/bright-forge-management/releases/latest**
2. Under **Assets**, tap the file ending in **`-android.apk`** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. Android will warn about installing unknown
   apps — tap **Settings → Allow from this source**, then go back and tap
   **Install**.
4. Open **Bright Forge Portal** from your home screen and log in.

You only ever do this once: the app loads the live portal, so every update
the team ships appears automatically the next time you open it.

> Requires portal version 1.0.308 or newer (older APKs bundled a frozen
> copy of the app; if you installed one before, uninstall it and install
> the latest).

## iPhone / iPad

Apple doesn't allow installing apps outside the App Store without a paid
developer account, so iPhones use the web app installed to the home
screen — it looks and behaves like a native app:

1. Open **https://echo-ai.tailfdbc33.ts.net** in **Safari**
   (must be Safari, not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open **Bright Forge** from your home screen and log in.

Updates are automatic — it always loads the live portal.

## Troubleshooting

- **App shows a blank page or an error**: the portal server may be down —
  check https://echo-ai.tailfdbc33.ts.net/healthz on any device (should
  say `ok`).
- **Android blocked the install**: Settings → Apps → Special app access →
  Install unknown apps → allow for Chrome (wording varies by phone).
- **Old Android app misbehaving**: uninstall it and install the latest APK
  from the releases link above.
